<Section>
  <TextBlock>
    <p className="type-label text-muted">05. Outcome &amp; Reflections</p>
    <h2 className="type-title text-block__heading">A More Flexible Ordering Experience.</h2>

      The revised concept responded to the usability feedback by addressing
      directly the insights pulled from the usability studies. It featured a
      saved restaurant feature, customisable shopping cart and facilitated for
      different payment methods.


      While the project concluded after one round of revisions only, it was
      nevertheless still important in imparting the values of a good research
      approach.

  </TextBlock>

  <TextBlock spaceBefore="8" desktopSpaceBefore="8">
    <h2 className="type-title text-block__heading">What I Took Away.</h2>

      This project was my first experience taking a mobile app concept through
      research, prototyping, usability testing, and design iteration. It showed
      me how a confusing label, a hard-to-find action, and the absence of a cart
      could all point to one larger issue: the app was simply not following the unspoken order
      that people had expected. As such, it only goes to show how a good user experience is more than just the visuals; it is also all about understanding the demographic that it strives to serve.

  </TextBlock>
