    <p className="type-label text-muted">04. Outcomes</p>
    <h2 id="outcomes-title" className="type-title text-block__heading">99% of test cases passed</h2>
    <p>Across system integration testing (SIT) and user acceptance testing (UAT), the test suite recorded a 99% pass rate. The remaining 1% exposed a system limitation that required specialist follow-up through a separate investigation.</p>
    <p>Broader coverage helped us surface issues before deployment. The unresolved case also showed why a high pass rate still needed to be read alongside the exceptions.</p>
    <p>Regression testing mattered because the recruitment workflow served <strong>20 stakeholder groups</strong>, with local differences in document generation and assessment stages. We checked both the new reference-check flow and existing paths, including cases ineligible for the enhancement.</p>

<Section aria-labelledby="takeaways-title">
  <TextBlock>
    <p className="type-label text-muted">05. Takeaways</p>
        <h3 className="type-title">What I would validate next</h3>
    <p>An acknowledged limitation would be that I was unable to conduct direct research with the platform users. Requirements and pain points came through client stakeholders, so my understanding of the experience relied on reported needs.</p>
    <p>In another iteration, I would like to observe recruiters carrying out reference checks and test the flow with them through open-ended user research studies, focusing on whether they understand when to initiate a check and what happens afterwards.</p>
    <p>I would also use the "time spent initiating checks" and the "need for manual follow-up" as part of my metrics. These qualitative and quantitative measures would help establish whether the enhancement did provide a significant drop in administrative efforts, or if the feature implementation was purely a perceived convenience.</p>
  </TextBlock>

  <TextBlock spaceBefore="8" desktopSpaceBefore="8">
    <h2 id="takeaways-title" className="type-title text-block__heading">Designing for continuity</h2>
    <p>This work taught me to treat inherited decisions, live data and organisational rules as part of the design problem. An improvement had to make sense within the workflows people already depended on.</p>
    <p>My contribution was to connect the user-facing requirement with the behaviour and testing needed to support it. That meant clarifying an incomplete handover, navigating stakeholder conversations and working closely with the client team under tight capacity constraints.</p>
    <Quote>
      <p><em>Good <strong>enterprise product design</strong> is not just about what users see. It is also about how clearly the data, rules and foundations behind that experience are crafted.</em></p>
    </Quote>
