    <div className="problem-cards">
      <article className="card problem-card flow">
        <span className="problem-card__number" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
        <h3 className="type-subtitle">Agree on requirements, then iterate</h3>
        <p>Late changes to the referee questionnaire had created repeated revisions and delayed development. Sequential handoffs made each change harder to absorb.</p>
        <p>We agreed on the key design requirements upfront so development could proceed alongside shorter feedback and testing cycles. This gave the team a clearer basis for implementation while allowing gaps to surface earlier.</p>
      </article>

      <article className="card problem-card flow">
        <span className="problem-card__number" aria-hidden="true">02</span>
        <h3 className="type-subtitle">Test the exceptions as well as the intended flow</h3>
        <p>The inherited test suite concentrated on the intended user journey. It offered less coverage of invalid inputs, alternative paths and effects on existing workflows.</p>
        <p>I pushed to expand the suite with positive, negative and regression cases. Testing alongside development helped us identify gaps while there was still time to address them.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

<section className="editorial-section editorial-section--research" aria-labelledby="outcomes-title">
  <div className="editorial-section__intro flow">
    <p className="editorial-eyebrow">04. Outcomes</p>
    <h2 id="outcomes-title" className="type-title">99% of test cases passed</h2>
    <p>Across system integration testing (SIT) and user acceptance testing (UAT), the test suite recorded a 99% pass rate. The remaining 1% exposed a system limitation that required specialist follow-up through a separate investigation.</p>
    <p>Broader coverage helped us surface issues before deployment. The unresolved case also showed why a high pass rate still needed to be read alongside the exceptions.</p>
    <p>Regression testing mattered because the recruitment workflow served <strong>20 agencies</strong>, with local differences in document generation and assessment stages. We checked both the new reference-check flow and existing paths, including cases ineligible for the enhancement.</p>
    <p>These results describe delivery validation. They do not establish how much time recruiters saved or how usable the new flow was in day-to-day work.</p>
  </div>

<section className="editorial-section editorial-section--outcome" aria-labelledby="takeaways-title">
  <div className="editorial-section__intro editorial-section__intro--storyboards flow">
    <p className="editorial-eyebrow">05. Takeaways</p>
    <h2 id="takeaways-title" className="type-title">Designing for continuity</h2>
    <p>This work taught me to treat inherited decisions, live data and organisational rules as part of the design problem. An improvement had to make sense within the workflows people already depended on.</p>
    <p>My contribution was to connect the user-facing requirement with the behaviour and testing needed to support it. That meant clarifying an incomplete handover, navigating stakeholder conversations and working closely with the client team under tight capacity constraints.</p>
    <blockquote className="editorial-quote">
      <p><em>In an established product, design quality includes how confidently a team can introduce change and maintain what comes next.</em></p>
    </blockquote>
  </div>

  <div className="editorial-section__intro editorial-section__intro--storyboards flow">
    <h3 className="type-title">What I would validate next</h3>
    <p>I could not conduct direct research with agency users. Requirements and pain points came through client stakeholders, so my understanding of the experience relied on reported needs.</p>
    <p>In another iteration, I would observe recruiters carrying out reference checks and test the flow with them, focusing on whether they understand when to initiate a check and what happens afterwards.</p>
    <p>I would also measure time spent initiating checks and the need for manual follow-up. Those measures would help establish whether the enhancement reduced administrative effort in practice.</p>
  </div>
